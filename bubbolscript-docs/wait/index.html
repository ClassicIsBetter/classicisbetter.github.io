1. Wait

Waiting is a very important part of scripting, once added to a script, the script will pause for a specified amount of time once it reaces a certain point

You can make a script wait by putting "wait [amount of time in seconds]" at a certain point

Here is an example of a script waiting before continuing to run

print printed instantly

wait 10

print printed after 10 seconds

Output:

printed instantly

-0:00

printed after 10 seconds

-0:10
